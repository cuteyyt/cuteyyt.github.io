Youtan Yin

PhD Student at NTU Singapore. Research Intern at Tencent AIPD.

I am a PhD student at the College of Computing and Data Science, Nanyang Technological University (NTU, Singapore), supervised by Prof. Lin Guosheng. I am currently also a Research Intern at Tencent AIPD.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University in 2022, where I worked closely with Prof. Song Mingli and Prof. Shen Chengchao.

Research Interests

My research focuses on 3D editing and reconstruction, with a broader interest in generative approaches for 3D content.
